# Executive Decision Memo: Customer Retention & MRR Leakage Mitigation

**TO:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Commercial Officer (CCO)  
**FROM:** Shivastha Rewaskar (Lead Analytics & Business Insights)  
**DATE:** October 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Diagnosis of \$2.28M Annualized Churn Leakage & Strategic Onboarding Overhaul  

---

### 1. Executive Summary
An in-depth analysis of 6,000 subscription accounts across 2025 revealed an aggregate customer churn rate of **25.67%**, accounting for **\$190,160 in lost Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**—representing an annualized run-rate loss of **\$2,281,920**. 

The primary catalyst for cancellation is not pricing or competitor switching, but **first-mile onboarding friction**: customers logging two or more technical support tickets within their first 30 days experience a **48.9% to 64.0% churn probability**, compared to under 19.5% for accounts with zero or one ticket. Implementing proactive customer success interventions can protect an estimated **\$540K in ARR** within 12 months.

---

### 2. Key Analytical Findings

#### Finding A: The "Two-Ticket" Cliff
* Accounts logging **0 to 1 support tickets** within 30 days retain at a stable rate (~81%).
* Reaching **2 support tickets** causes churn to jump dramatically to **48.93%**.
* At **3+ support tickets**, churn peaks at **62.0% – 64.0%**, directly causing \$96,550 in annualized MRR loss.
* *Root Cause:* 45.32% of all churned clients explicitly cited *"Unresolved Technical Issues / Support Latency"* as their cancellation reason.

#### Finding B: Acquisition Channel Quality Discrepancies
* **Paid Social** exhibits the highest churn rate at **38.10%** (\$71,719 in MRR lost), accompanied by lower product adoption scores (avg. 41/100).
* **Referral** channels exhibit a low **14.93% churn rate**, generating the highest Customer Lifetime Value (\$997 per account).

### 4. Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

1. **Deploy Automated "Red-Flag" Ticket Triage (Immediate - 30 Days):**
   * Automatically escalate any customer account that files a 2nd support ticket within 30 days to Senior Support Engineers with a guaranteed 2-hour SLA.
2. **Reallocate Digital Acquisition Budget (Quarter 1):**
   * Reduce Paid Social ad spend by 25% and reallocate \$40,000 toward Referral incentive schemes and high-intent Organic Content marketing.
3. **Product In-App Guided Onboarding (Quarter 1-2):**
   * Replace manual PDF setup documentation with an interactive in-app wizard to prevent early configuration failure points.
